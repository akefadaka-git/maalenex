# MaaleNex Future Scope Register v1.0

**Status:** Future-scope specification only — not authorized for public exposure or implementation in the passenger preview.

## Motorcycle-driver capabilities
Potential future capabilities include application intake, identity and licence evidence, eligibility review, vehicle linkage, availability, service acceptance, safety workflows, earnings display, and support. These require legal classification, licensing, insurance, safety controls, server-side identity and role authorization, auditability, and Owner approval before implementation.

## Heavy-load and cargo capabilities
Potential future capabilities include cargo request intake, load characteristics, vehicle capacity, route restrictions, loading/unloading, delivery evidence, insurance, and dispute handling. This domain requires separate operational, regulatory, insurance, and safety assessment. It is not a passenger registration option.

## Fleet and transport-operator capabilities
Potential future capabilities include organization onboarding, delegated staff, vehicle/driver rosters, service monitoring, compliance evidence, and operational reporting. Access must be tenant-isolated and granted only through server-side authorization. No public role selector may grant fleet or operator privileges.

## Common exit gates
Before any future domain enters implementation:
- A1 requirements and legal/operational scope approved.
- A2 read-only architecture, data, and dependency reconciliation completed.
- A3 implementation separately authorized.
- A4 security, privacy, accessibility, role-isolation, and operational verification passed.
- A5 deployment and commercial activation separately approved.

Until those gates are met, these capabilities remain specifications only. Their mention does not imply service availability, licensing, partnership, or authorization.
