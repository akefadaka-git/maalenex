# Cargo Operational-Volume Planning Baseline — Owner-Approved Provisional Assumptions

**Status:** OWNER-APPROVED PROVISIONAL PLANNING BASELINE  
**Effective record date:** 23 November 2026  
**Scope:** MaaleNex Cargo Delivery, hybrid service model C  
**Classification:** Planning assumptions only — NOT ACTUAL VOLUMES, NOT A LEGAL THRESHOLD, NOT A DPO DETERMINATION.

## 1. Owner decision

The Project Owner approved recording the following staged operating-volume assumptions for planning and for the D03/D04 evidence-gap work. These figures are a provisional capacity-planning scenario. They are not observed activity, a launch commitment, a revenue forecast, a regulatory safe harbour or a legal interpretation of “large scale”.

## 2. Provisional planning scenario

| Metric | Initial launch | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Average deliveries per operating day | 10 | 30 | 75 |
| Peak deliveries per operating day | 20 | 60 | 150 |
| Operating days per month | 26 | 26 | 26 |
| Approximate deliveries per month (average-day basis) | 260 | 780 | 1,950 |
| Approximate deliveries per year (average-day basis) | 3,120 | 9,360 | 23,400 |

**Calculation:** average deliveries/day × 26 operating days/month; monthly estimate × 12 months. Peak-day values are capacity-planning peaks, not average-volume multipliers.

## 3. Scope and allocation rules

- Figures represent the combined MaaleNex Cargo Delivery operating scenario across the hybrid service model.
- No numerical split between MaaleNex-operated deliveries (SL-01) and independent partner-operated deliveries (SL-02) has been approved. Do not invent or infer one.
- The scenario does not establish the number of unique senders, recipients, couriers, partner organisations, staff or other data subjects.
- It does not establish tracking events per delivery, coordinate-bearing event percentage, coordinate source/frequency/duration, evidence-upload rate/size, exception rate, geographic reach or growth distribution.
- Those measures remain separately required for D03/D04 and Article 40 analysis.

## 4. Data-protection and DPO qualification

These delivery counts do not determine whether Article 40 applies. Article 40's government-body, large-scale regular and systematic monitoring, and large-scale sensitive-personal-data limbs are alternative tests. A relatively small delivery operation could still trigger a limb depending on the nature, purpose, scope, duration, frequency, population and sensitivity of processing. Conversely, delivery counts alone do not prove that a limb is triggered.

Accordingly:
- D02 remains **IN PROGRESS / UNDETERMINED** for the monitoring and sensitive-data limbs.
- No conclusion that a DPO is mandatory or unnecessary is made.
- No DPO appointment or non-appointment is authorised by this planning record.
- The government-body limb remains not triggered on current evidence, subject to reassessment if a government-body arrangement is introduced.

## 5. Operating-control assumptions recommended for planning

The planning scenario assumes a controlled, low-monitoring launch design, subject to separate product, legal and Owner approvals:
1. Use discrete, event-based tracking; do not introduce continuous GPS polling by implication.
2. Collect coordinates only where a documented operational purpose justifies them; record source, precision, access and retention.
3. Do not introduce automated courier scoring, behavioural profiling or risk scoring without a separate assessment and approval.
4. Minimise delivery-proof content; prohibit unnecessary identity documents and sensitive details; provide a classification/escalation process for unexpected content.
5. Measure actual activity monthly and refresh the forecast quarterly, retaining the measurement period, definitions, source, service-line split, owner and approval history.

These are planning controls, not evidence that the controls are implemented or operationally effective.

## 6. Evidence and change control

Before this scenario can support a final D02/D03/D04 conclusion, record:
- actual unique sender/recipient and courier/staff subject counts;
- actual deliveries and dispatches by service line, average month and peak day;
- tracking events per dispatch, coordinate-bearing share, coordinate source/frequency/duration and retention;
- evidence uploads, file-size distribution and content-classification outcomes;
- exceptions, geography, partner/courier counts and material growth assumptions;
- source system, measurement period, extraction date, data-quality limitations and accountable approver.

Replace or supplement this scenario only through a dated, attributable revision. Preserve this initial Owner-approved baseline as a historical planning decision. Any revised forecast must remain clearly distinguished from measured actuals.

## 7. Governance boundary

This approval records planning assumptions only. It does not approve external submission, regulatory filing, partner contracting, courier operations, live personal-data processing, production activation, code changes, merge, deployment, purge/retention automation, DPO appointment or A5 activity. All remain subject to their separate evidence and authorization gates.
